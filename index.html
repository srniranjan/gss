Education, health and environment — for rural Karnataka

Guru Shishya Sangama Public Charitable Trust was founded in 2024 by eighteen retired officers, doctors, teachers, farmers and professionals to support students who would otherwise go without.

What we do

The Trust exists to take a holistic approach to education, sports, health, art, music and the environment. In practice that means concessional schooling for poor students, direct support to talented and disabled students, seminars, relief work, and encouraging rural boys and girls to stay in education.
