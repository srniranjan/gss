Core objectives
Our mission

A holistic approach to learning

Concessional schooling for poor students, direct support to talented and disabled students, seminars, relief work, and encouraging rural boys and girls to stay in education — through art, music, sport and the environment too.

Our vision

Begin humbly. Grow into an institution.

To add one faculty at a time and grow into an institution that serves rural students in education, health, sports, art, music and the environment.
