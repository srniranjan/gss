Our Vision

To begin humbly, add one faculty at a time, and grow into an institution that serves rural students in education, health, sports, art, music and the environment.

The founding intent

As set out in the deed, the Trust “envisages a long drawn out plan which can start its work with a humble beginning and go on adding many alternative system faculties, co-ordinating them and developing a fine institution.”

It is a public charitable trust with no commercial or profit motive, and it is irrevocable. Its funds and income are applied solely to the objectives below.

Registered objectives

These are the eight objectives the Trust is legally bound to, as recorded in clause 6 of the registered trust deed.

  1. To provide a holistic approach towards education, sports, health, art, music and environment.
  2. To establish, construct and maintain educational institutions at concessional rates for poor students.
  3. To provide educational support to talented, disabled and deserving students.
  4. To promote education and research in the field of education, sports, health and environment.
  5. To conduct seminars on education-related programmes.
  6. To carry on social service or relief work for the poor, disabled and others — on its own or with similar organisations.
  7. To encourage boys and girls from rural areas to continue their education, including art and music.
  8. To participate in and implement central and state government schemes related to education.
Who benefits. The deed states the benefit of the Trust shall extend to all sections of the public in Karnataka and in India at large, without any distinction.
